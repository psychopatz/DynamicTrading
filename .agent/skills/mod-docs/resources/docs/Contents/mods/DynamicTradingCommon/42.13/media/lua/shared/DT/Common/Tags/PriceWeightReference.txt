Title: Tags Price and Weight Reference
Tags: [chunk, internal]

LOCATION: Contents/mods/DynamicTradingCommon/42.13/media/lua/shared/DT/Common/Tags.lua
PURPOSE: Explain how tag price multipliers and spawn weights drive economy behavior.

TAG VALUE MODEL:
- `priceMult`: modifies final trade price contribution for the tag.
- `weight`: influences random selection probability for stock generation.
- Combined item price = base price multiplied by all relevant tag effects and system modifiers.

RARITY EXAMPLE MODEL:
- Common: price 1.0, weight high.
- Uncommon: price around 1.25, weight moderate.
- Rare: price around 2.0, weight low.
- Legendary: price very high, weight minimal.

CATEGORY EXAMPLE MODEL:
- Medical/Ammo generally carry higher price multipliers and lower weights.
- Food/Drink tend toward baseline pricing and higher availability.
- Fuel/resources may have scarcity premiums due to survival demand.

CONDITIONAL TAGS:
- Tags such as `Winter`, `Harvest`, `Illegal` may be set to non-random spawn behavior.
- They are often introduced through event injection or specialized archetypes.

DESIGN NOTES:
- Avoid root-level collisions (for example `Luxury` should be under `Quality.Luxury`).
- Keep hierarchy consistent (`Root.SubType.Detail`) for reliable matching.
- Rebalance with both `priceMult` and `weight`; changing one in isolation can destabilize markets.
