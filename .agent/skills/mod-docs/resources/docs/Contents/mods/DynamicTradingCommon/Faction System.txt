# Faction System (V1/V2)

Factions represent organized groups in the world with their own resources and reputations.

## Key Concepts
- **Soul Persistence:** Faction state (Stable, Starving, Booming) is persistent.
- **Reputation:** Players gain or lose favor with factions through trading and quests.
- **Resource Consumption:** Factions consume their stockpiles daily, driving the need for trade.

## Synergy
Whether you interact with a faction representative via the radio or in person, your reputation and the faction's economic state remain consistent.
